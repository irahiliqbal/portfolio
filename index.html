Manifesto

The founder.

I was raised in Ghazipur, Uttar Pradesh—a quiet city defining the banks of the Ganga. At 19, I relocated to Chandigarh to pursue a B.E. in Computer Science at CU Mohali.

I do not build software for the sake of software. I engineer solutions that create real-world impact. An AI-powered IoT irrigation system that helps farmers prevent crop loss through intelligent water management, a blockchain-based credential platform that ensures tamper-proof academic verification, and digital platforms that simplify access to services through efficient, user-centric design. For me, software is not just code; it is a tool for solving practical problems and driving meaningful human progress.

Graduating in 2025, I immediately pivoted to founding ProjectAds to radically democratize an opaque ₹3,500 Cr industry. When I am not architecting systems or talking to customers, I am traveling to new places, 3D printing stuff, exploring the real world with FPV drones, or cooking something new.
